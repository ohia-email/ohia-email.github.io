A native macOS email client · Everything runs on your Mac

Your correspondence, reorganized by meaning.

Ohia reads your mailbox as a field of relationships rather than a stack of folders. Every message becomes a node, placed by what it says, who it involves, and when it happened. Search, summaries, questions, translation, and personalized drafting all run on your Mac.

Enter anywhere. Each lit node is a way in, and none of them is the front door.

Origins

The mailbox outlived every client that made it.

Unix mail clients have been named for trees since 1986. Elm was electronic mail. Pine was, by its own name, not Elm. Alpine carried Pine forward when the University of Washington set it down. Across all of them, and across Eudora, Mutt, and every graphical client since, one thing never changed: the mailbox. Inbox, Sent, folders, and the protocol that serves them are the same architecture MH was scripting at RAND in 1979.

1979MHMail Handler, at RAND. One file per message, driven from the shell.
1986ElmELectronic Mail. One of the first full-screen text clients, and the first tree.
1988EudoraNamed for Eudora Welty. Its promise: bringing the P.O. to where you live.
1989Pine“Pine Is Not Elm,” later “Program for Internet News and E-mail.”
1995MuttElm's interface rewritten from scratch, with threading and scoring.
2007AlpinePine carried forward after the University of Washington ended it in 2006.
2026OhiaThe ʻōhiʻa lehua. A field of nodes, placed by meaning. No mailbox in sight.
The mailboxInbox · Sent · folders · mbox → IMAP. One architecture, unbroken from 1979 to today.
The field begins.

Why a tree, and why this one

Ohia keeps the tree tradition and moves it to Hawaiʻi. The ʻōhiʻa lehua is endemic to the islands and is usually the first plant to grow on new lava. Its flower has no petals to speak of: it is a burst of stamens, each tipped with a point of yellow. Once every message becomes a node, that is what a mailbox looks like.

What the letters expand to

PINE was a recursive acronym: “Pine Is Not Elm.” Ohia is one too: “Ohia Hates Inbox Architecture.” Pine later took a formal expansion, “Program for Internet News and E-mail.” Ohia’s is “Ontological Hypergraph of Intelligent Associations,” a graph of messages whose meaning lives in their associations.

The step past the mailbox

Eudora brought the post office to where you live. Ohia's proposal is that the post office was never the right shape for what mail became. IMAP is read as a legacy format: raw material to read from, never to replicate. The mailbox stays intact on your server. Nothing in the interface is a folder.

Frames

Five frames on one field. None of them is the home screen.

The same correspondence arranged five ways, rendered natively with Metal. No frame is the root of the others and every one is a place to begin. Change frame and the nodes move to new positions; nothing is filed, moved, or hidden.

Digest

Newest and most consequential mail first, as articles and conversation packages. A thread collapses into one card and opens in place. The reader shows the whole message.

Meanings

Messages settle near what they resemble. Dense regions become clusters, drawn as lit gas around their members with a spiked nucleus. Clusters form, hold, and dissolve as mail arrives.

Correspondents

Each person is a small solar system. Orbit distance is recency, speed is urgency, and eccentricity is how evenly the exchange runs both ways. A role is a weighted distribution, never a single label.

Projects

You define a project as a rule: which correspondents, tags, or keywords belong to it. Ohia matches new mail against the rule and recommends related tags as the thread grows. Every project gets a Gantt view, a step-by-step deadline guide, and an Ohia calendar in Apple Calendar.

Timeline

Messages along the date they were sent, with a diverging histogram: received above the line, sent below. Time presets follow the mail's own dates, not when it happened to arrive.

Layered perception

Content first. Interpretation is an overlay.

Lock a node and the message is the primary object. Interpretation sits on top, on demand: weighted dimensions, evidence highlighted in the text, the neighborhood of related nodes. Every label traces back to a span in the message. When signals conflict, the conflict stays visible instead of being settled behind the scenes.

Traceable, not oracular

Every dimension shows the spans that produced it. There is no label without a source, and no “the system decided” anywhere in the interface.

Identity as a distribution

A correspondent is not “a coworker.” They are weights across roles that shift as the exchange changes, and the weights are shown as weights.

Failure as structure

Uncertainty degrades into visible structure: low-confidence signals, a split interpretation, missing data. Never silence, never false certainty.

Composer

A draft is recomposed from precedent. It is not spoken on your behalf.

Before a single question is asked, you curate the sources a reply may draw on. Composer then asks what only you can answer, in short rounds that adapt to your previous answers. The draft is assembled from those answers, the cited sources, and the register your own sent mail established. Every component is visible and separable, and the draft is yours to change before it goes anywhere.

Send

Registers form on their own

Your sent mail separates into style registers per language. The register you use with a colleague in Japanese is not the one you use with a vendor in English, and Composer keeps them apart.

Grounded means grounded

Only facts present in the curated sources can enter a draft, and every one carries a citation back to its message. The grounding footer counts what the draft may rely on.

You remain the sender

Accuracy, tone, recipients, attachments, and the decision to send stay with you. Quoted history is re-attached mechanically at send and never used to shape the draft.

Continuous training

Trains in the background. Reports as measurements.

Ohia fine-tunes a local model on your eligible sent mail, a little at a time, in the background — while you are in another app, or whenever the Mac goes idle. It steps aside the moment you are actively drafting in Composer, and pauses on battery, in Low Power Mode, or under thermal or memory pressure. Training resumes from checkpoints. Progress is reported as loss, corpus coverage, and per-draft influence, never as a mind getting smarter.

Ask

Questions answered with citations

Ask a question of the mail within the scope you have set. The answer is generated on your Mac and every sentence cites the messages it came from. Follow a citation and its node lights up in the field.

Search

Find what you remember, not what you filed

Look for the discussion you remember without its subject line. A local semantic index places messages by meaning across languages, so a query in English can surface the reply that was written in Japanese.

Translation

Read every message in your language

Translation runs on your Mac, with your downloaded generative model or Apple's on-device translation. A glossary pins your own renderings of recurring terms, so a name or a title is translated the same way every time.

Memories

Structure that forms, holds, and dissolves.

From your threads Ohia distills structured claims: what was decided, what is owed, what a term means to a particular correspondent. Each claim carries its evidence and a recency weight. Claims that stop being reinforced decay and disappear. Dossiers accumulate per correspondent, project, category, account, and translation target, and every one can be inspected, corrected, or deleted.

Evidence or nothing

A claim exists only while messages support it. Each one links to the spans it came from; contradicted claims are superseded rather than silently overwritten.

Injected selectively

When you draft or ask, only the claims relevant to that correspondent, project, or language enter the context. You can see which ones did.

The one metaphor

Ohia calls this background distillation dreaming. It is the single figure of speech the interface allows itself, and even there it shows the gauge.

Where your data goes

Local is an architecture, not a promise.

Mailbox copies, indexes, summaries, drafts, memories, and the personalized model live on your Mac. Inference and training use your Mac's own processors. Credentials and authorization tokens stay in the macOS Keychain. Ingestion is read-only: Ohia changes your mailbox only when you ask it to send, file, flag, or delete a message.

Ohia does not sell email data or use it for advertising. Your email is not used to train a shared or general-purpose model; personalization belongs to your local workspace. Local files rely on macOS account and filesystem protections and are not all separately encrypted by Ohia.
