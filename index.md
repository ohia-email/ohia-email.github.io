# Ohia

## Email, with context.

Ohia is a native macOS email client that brings your correspondence, the people behind it, and your writing into one workspace.

Read and send email, find the conversation you need, explore connections between messages, and prepare replies grounded in your correspondence. Search, summaries, and personalized drafting run on your Mac.

### Your correspondence, connected

- **Read and write.** Work with your mailbox, compose replies, and manage drafts through your email provider.
- **Find the context.** Search your mail and explore conversations, people, projects, and timelines.
- **Make sense of your mail.** Use local intelligence to summarize messages and surface relevant information.
- **Draft in your own voice.** Ohia learns from your sent correspondence to personalize a model for your use on your Mac. You review your drafts before sending.

### Know where your data goes

Connecting an account gives Ohia access to the mailbox information needed to provide its email features. Mail and derived information are stored locally. Account credentials are kept in the macOS Keychain.

Email is exchanged with your provider. Optional connected sources and web research can send requests, including questions derived from a message, to services you enable. The [Privacy Policy](/privacy/) explains these connections, personalization, and your controls.

### Availability

Ohia is in active development for macOS. Provider authorization and release preparation are underway; this site does not offer a public app download yet.
