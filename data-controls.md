# Manage and delete your data

These controls describe the current development build of Ohia. Read the [Privacy Policy](/privacy/) for the information each connection uses.

## Stop account access

You can revoke Ohia's authorization from your email provider's account settings. For Google, use [your Google Account connections](https://myaccount.google.com/connections) to remove Ohia's access. For a provider using an app-specific password, revoke that password in the provider's account settings.

Revocation prevents continued authorized access. It does not delete mail already downloaded to your Mac or messages held by your provider.

## Remove Ohia's local data

In the current build, open **Ohia Settings → Developer → Reset → Application → Reset Ohia**. Review the confirmation before proceeding. Ohia exits, and its private state is cleared during the reset on the next launch.

The reset removes Ohia's account configuration and credentials, local message copies, attachments, drafts, summaries, memories, search indexes, caches, and personalized training state. Unsent work stored in Ohia is included, so save anything you need first.

The reset leaves your provider's mailbox and manual exports outside Ohia unchanged. It may retain verified public model downloads. Copies in device backups remain subject to your backup controls.

## Reset personalization

**Ohia Settings → Developer → Reset → Training personalization → Reset Training to Base** deletes personalization checkpoints. It is not a permanent opt-out: automatic training can create new personalization from eligible sent correspondence. Use the full application reset when your purpose is to remove Ohia's private data.

## Control connected sources

In **Ohia Settings → Sources**, disable automatic research, disable a source, or remove its connection. You can also revoke its authorization at the connected service. Disconnecting a service does not erase requests it has already received; contact that provider about its retention and deletion controls.

## Support correspondence

To request deletion of information you sent to Ohia support, contact [jgkarlin@gmail.com](mailto:jgkarlin@gmail.com). Identify the request or correspondence to locate, without including passwords or additional private email.
